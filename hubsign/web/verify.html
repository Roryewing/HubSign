HubSign

Verify a signed document

Drop a PDF to check that it still matches what HubSign sealed. Your file is checked on this server and is not stored.

What this proves

A green result means the file is byte-for-byte what HubSign sealed on the date shown: no signature, page or text was changed afterwards. It does not by itself prove who the signers were. That is recorded in the certificate of completion at the end of the PDF, including how each signer was verified.

Adobe Acrobat can also check the seal. If it says the signer is unknown, add this certificate as a trusted identity, or ask the sender whether they use a certificate from a recognised authority.
